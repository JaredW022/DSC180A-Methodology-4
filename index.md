**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?** \
The most interesting topic for me that we have covered in this domain was regarding the evaluation of association on SNPs and gene expression. From a LocusZoom plot, we are able to determine "peaks" within our chromosome vs -log p value graph, informing us which SNPs have statistically significant effects. Overlaying this against a particular trait or disease, we may see similarities between the peaks, and associate a relationship between the two. However, in practice, the most statistically significant SNP may not be causal, but rather, be capturing the effect of another SNP, or perhaps, even the joint effect of multiple different SNPs.
